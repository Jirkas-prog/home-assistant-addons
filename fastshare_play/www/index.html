Library

Use folders inside /media or /share. The player never changes your movie files.

Newer sidecar progress is imported. Browser progress is saved here; media sidecars are never modified.

Movie previews

Frames are taken from the middle 60% of each film and saved in the add-on. One film is processed at a time. No accounts or API keys are needed.

Player on your network

The hostname builds the link below. Configure your own DNS record to point to Home Assistant.

About

A private movie library with browser playback. Built in the WorkFlow visual style.

Open-source credits

Selected metadata and resume logic is adapted from NOVA Video Player / Archos (Apache 2.0). The web interface and server are a separate implementation.

NOVA sourceFastShare Play sourceApache 2.0NOTICE

Local preview extraction uses FFmpeg as a separate command-line program, supplied by Debian. No movie data is sent to a metadata service.

Playback compatibility

Video and audio are decoded by your browser. There is no transcoding. Some MKV, AVI, HEVC, DTS or subtitle formats require a compatible external player. MP4 with H.264 and AAC is the most widely supported combination.

Watch state and favorites are shared by this household library.
