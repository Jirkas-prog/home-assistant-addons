Media library

One folder per line, inside /media or /share. The download folder is always included. Existing videos stay in place.

Videos play directly in your browser without conversion. MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio is widely supported. Other formats depend on your browser. Matching .srt and .vtt subtitle files appear in the player.

◉ MQTT and diagnostics

Connecting

Suggested shared add-on topic: mqtt_addon. FastShare publishes under mqtt_addon/fastshare. Automatic connection uses the internal broker, usually port 1883. MQTT is required for the dashboard card.

Your own live card.

Its own web server and widget. No additional dashboard add-on.

Add to Home Assistant

Choose a dashboard to append a new FastShare tab. The card is registered automatically and a backup is saved before any change. You can then move or copy the card anywhere.

Make it your size

Choose Auto or 2, 3, 4 rows. Compact cards keep counts visible and show fewer transfers. You can change the height in the Home Assistant card editor.
