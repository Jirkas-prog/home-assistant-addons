◉ MQTT and diagnostics

Connecting

Suggested shared add-on topic: mqtt_addon. FastShare publishes under mqtt_addon/fastshare. Automatic connection uses the internal broker, usually port 1883. MQTT is required for the dashboard card.

YOUR STATUS, WHERE YOU WANT IT

Your own live card.

Its own web server and widget. No additional dashboard add-on.

Add to Home Assistant

Choose a dashboard to append a new FastShare tab. The card is registered automatically and a backup is saved before any change. You can then move or copy the card anywhere.

Make it your size

The card adapts to the Home Assistant grid and sizes itself to its content. Only longer download lists scroll inside the card.
